What to include in your message

Five short answers are enough for me to scope your project properly in the first reply.

1

Your business

What you sell or serve, and where — a restaurant chain, a farm, a retail shop and a marketplace all need different systems.

2

What it must manage

Orders, stock, bookings, staff, customers, deliveries — name the processes the software has to run every day.

3

Who will use it

How many staff, on what devices — counter PCs, phones in the field, an office dashboard — and whether it must work offline.

4

What exists today

Your current website or software, if any, and what is wrong with it. Working systems get improved, not replaced, wherever that is honest.

5

Your timeline

When you need it live, and any fixed date driving that — a launch, a season, a campaign.

6

Then what happens

I reply with a plan, a timeline and a price. If I am not the right person for the work, I say so plainly — before you have spent anything.
