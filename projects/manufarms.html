Overview

Manu Farms runs farm operations in Bangladesh — a business where the work happens in fields and sheds, far from any office, and where records kept on paper arrive too late to act on. The company's answer was to run its operations digitally, and manufarms.com is the platform at the center of that effort.

As I.T & Digital Operations Lead I have worked on the farm-management platform itself — the system the business uses to run day-to-day operations digitally and at scale — and on the company's wider digital operations: its online presence, content, and the practical tooling the team depends on.

The platform is also the public home of the Poultry Manager application: the site presents the app to farmers, documents its features and IoT devices, and carries the company's announcements — including its BRAC Bank MoU, which opened SME loan access for farmers using the app. Working here taught me a durable lesson: software for agriculture succeeds only when it fits the connectivity, devices and habits of the people on the farm, not the people in the office.

The work is deliberately practical: records that field staff will actually keep, pages that load on rural connections, and tools the team reaches for without being trained twice. A farm platform earns its place season by season, and this one has — it remains the system the company organises its operations and its public presence around. My role covers both the platform's direction and its daily upkeep — a combination that suits a business where the office and the field are effectively the same team.

What it includes

  • Public platform site presenting the business and its products
  • Poultry Manager's home — app presentation, features and IoT devices
  • Digital operations — presence, content and team tooling
