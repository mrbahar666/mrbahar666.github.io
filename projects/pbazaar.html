Overview

pbazaar.com is one of the large-scale e-commerce platforms in Bangladesh — a high-traffic store where performance and reliability are not polish but preconditions: slow pages and broken flows translate directly into lost orders at volume.

As IT Officer for web development, my work has covered the core platform and its evolution, including the V2 update and a front-end redesign. The platform is built on nopCommerce and the .NET stack — C#, ASP.NET and Microsoft SQL Server — a different ecosystem from my PHP and JavaScript work, and one that rewards careful, conservative change: on a store of this size, you verify everything twice before it ships.

Alongside development I have handled the platform's ongoing digital operations — the day-to-day work of keeping a large catalogue storefront correct, current and selling. It is the role that taught me how e-commerce behaves at scale: caching, catalogue hygiene, and the operational discipline behind a storefront that thousands of customers treat as routine.

The front-end redesign was driven by how customers actually shop a large catalogue: clearer category structure, faster product discovery, and pages that stay light even when a category carries hundreds of items. Working inside an established platform also means respecting what already converts — changes ship incrementally, measured against the live store's behaviour rather than taste. That discipline — small, verified steps on a system that cannot afford a bad day — is the same one I bring to every platform on this page, whatever the stack. It is also where my dual background — platform engineering and digital operations — is most useful.

What it includes

  • Core platform development on nopCommerce / .NET
  • V2 platform update
  • Front-end redesign of the storefront
  • Performance work for a high-traffic catalogue store
  • Digital operations — keeping a large store correct and current daily
